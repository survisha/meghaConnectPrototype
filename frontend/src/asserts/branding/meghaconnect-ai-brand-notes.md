# MEGHACONNECT AI Brand Assets

## Concept

The mark keeps the existing MeghaConnect two-person M identity and upgrades the center join into an AI bridge. The left figure represents the citizen, the right figure represents the CM Office / government side, and the center neural bridge represents the AI intelligence layer.

## Symbolism

Citizen <-> AI <-> Government

- Two human figures: public trust and service access
- Modern M shape: continuity with MeghaConnect
- Center infinity bridge: always-on digital connection
- Neural nodes: AI-assisted routing, grievance intelligence, scheme tracking, and engagement
- White glow: confidence, clarity, and responsiveness
